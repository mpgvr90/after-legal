# AFTER Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** TETIANA LAHOTSKA · **Contact:** [codexxdev77@gmail.com](mailto:codexxdev77@gmail.com)  
**Last updated:** 10 October 2026

AFTER is a private recovery journal. Your journal entries, photos, check-ins, reasons, letters, and preferences are stored on your device. We do not operate an account or a server that receives this content. An export or backup leaves your device only when you choose to save or share it. Backups are encrypted with the passphrase you choose; we cannot recover that passphrase.

## Purchases and trial

AFTER uses RevenueCat to check purchase and trial status. RevenueCat assigns an anonymous app user identifier and processes technical information about the device and app, IP address, purchase records and store transaction data. RevenueCat does not receive your journal entries or photos through this integration. Its processing is described in the [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy-policy/).

On Android, the free trial starts when RevenueCat first sees the app user. On iOS, the five-day trial starts when you claim the free trial in the App Store. The app uses RevenueCat's purchase and transaction dates to decide when access ends. Trial information is used for access control, not advertising.

The App Store or Google Play processes payments under its own terms and privacy rules. We receive purchase status through RevenueCat, not your card details. Purchases can be restored from within AFTER.

## Device features

If you enable notifications, AFTER schedules them on your device. If you enable biometric lock, authentication is handled by your device; AFTER does not receive biometric templates. Home-screen widgets can show limited recovery information according to your settings. You can delete app data from the app or device settings. Store and RevenueCat transaction records may remain under their own retention rules.

## Your choices and contact

You can disable notifications, widgets, and optional lock features in device or app settings. To ask about purchase data or privacy, contact [codexxdev77@gmail.com](mailto:codexxdev77@gmail.com). For purchase or refund requests, use the App Store or Google Play process.
